# MASTER PROMPT – SRI VENKATESWARA SWAMY TEMPLE WEBSITE

## Project identity

**Temple:** Sri Venkateswara Swamy Temple (also associated with the name Kovai Tirupati)  
**Address:** 22QF+996, Peelamedu, B.R. Puram Industrial Estate, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu 641004, India  
**Google Maps:** https://maps.google.com/maps?ftid=0x3ba857778f285347:0xb044aa35ac6c1d9f  
**Contact:** +91 96777 47282 *(verify with temple administration before publishing)*

## 1. Project vision

Create a premium, production-quality, mobile-first devotional website for Sri Venkateswara Swamy Temple.

The website should feel like an official temple digital experience—not a generic business website. Its visual identity should be rooted in South Indian Vaishnava temple traditions while offering the usability and polish of a modern mobile website.

**Mobile devices are the primary target.** Design for phones first, then adapt the experience elegantly to tablets and desktops.

## 2. Design direction

**Concept:** Divine Heritage — Traditional South Indian Temple + Modern Premium Experience.

### Colour palette
- Deep maroon
- Antique gold
- Warm ivory
- Temple red
- Subtle dark brown

### Visual language
- Authentic photographs of this temple and its deity.
- Traditional South Indian ornamental motifs and borders.
- Elegant serif headings paired with a highly readable body font.
- Calm, devotional layouts with generous spacing.
- Subtle transitions and animations.
- High-quality image treatment without making photographs look artificial.

Avoid neon colours, generic corporate layouts, excessive gradients, clutter, unnecessary glassmorphism and distracting animations.

## 3. Homepage

Build a visually memorable homepage containing:

1. **Mobile header**
   - Temple identity/logo.
   - Compact navigation menu.
   - Clear, accessible controls.

2. **Hero section**
   - Authentic, approved photograph of Lord Venkateswara Swamy or the temple.
   - Temple name: Sri Venkateswara Swamy Temple.
   - A short devotional greeting, such as “Om Namo Narayanaya”.
   - Primary actions: “Explore Temple” and “Get Directions”.
   - Ensure text remains legible over the image.

3. **Quick links**
   - Darshan Timings
   - Pooja & Seva
   - Festivals
   - Gallery
   - Contact & Location

4. **Temple introduction**
   - A concise introduction and a link to the About page.

5. **Darshan information**
   - Display administration-approved opening and darshan timings.
   - Clearly indicate that timings may change on festival days.

6. **Festivals and announcements**
   - Highlight upcoming events.
   - Display dates only after confirmation.
   - Make announcements easy for the temple team to update.

7. **Photo gallery preview**
   - Use real temple photographs.
   - Link to the full gallery.

8. **Location and contact preview**
   - Show the address and approved contact details.
   - Provide directions through Google Maps.

9. **Footer**
   - Temple name, address, contact, useful links and a devotional closing message.

## 4. Website pages and sections

### A. Home
A premium devotional landing page with quick access to the most important temple information.

### B. About the Temple
Include:
- Temple introduction.
- Presiding deity details.
- Temple architecture and features.
- Sthala Puranam or temple history, if verified and approved.
- Relevant traditions and significance.

Do not invent historical dates, legends or religious claims. Use verified information or content approved by temple management.

### C. Darshan Timings
Present morning and evening timings in a clear, easy-to-read format. Include a notice that timings may change during festivals or special occasions. Confirm all timings with the temple before publishing.

### D. Pooja & Seva
Display only services confirmed by the temple administration. Potential information to verify includes:
- Available poojas and sevas.
- Offerings and prasadam.
- Tonsuring facilities.
- Thulabaram facilities.
- Booking procedure, if available.
- Fees or charges, only when officially confirmed.

Do not create a payment or booking flow unless the temple explicitly authorizes it.

### E. Festivals & Events
Create elegant event cards with:
- Festival or event name.
- Confirmed date and time.
- Short description.
- Relevant approved photographs.
- Important announcements.

Include festivals such as Vaikunta Ekadasi or other celebrations only where applicable and confirmed. Make event dates editable.

### F. Gallery
Create a mobile-friendly gallery for:
- Temple exterior and architecture.
- Main deity.
- Alankaram and flower decorations.
- Festivals and celebrations.
- Temple activities and events.

Use authentic, high-resolution temple images. Do not present unrelated temples or generic deity images as photographs of this temple.

### G. Contact & Location
Display:
- Verified temple address.
- Confirmed phone number.
- Google Maps directions.
- Approved contact or enquiry options.
- Temple opening information, where verified.

## 5. Mobile-first user experience

The mobile experience must receive the highest design and testing priority.

Requirements:
- Responsive layout designed from a phone-sized viewport upward.
- Fixed or sticky bottom navigation, if it improves usability.
- Large, touch-friendly buttons and links.
- One-tap Google Maps directions.
- One-tap phone calling.
- WhatsApp contact only if the temple approves the number and use.
- Smooth scrolling and restrained animations.
- No horizontal overflow.
- Proper spacing around iPhone safe areas.
- Readable text without zooming.
- Fast-loading, appropriately sized images.
- Accessible contrast and clear focus states.
- Simple navigation that works comfortably with one hand.

Do not overload the homepage. Keep the most frequently needed information easy to find.

## 6. Technical requirements

Use a modern, maintainable frontend stack appropriate to the project.

- Build reusable components and consistent design tokens.
- Use semantic HTML and accessible controls.
- Optimize images with WebP or AVIF where supported.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold images.
- Avoid unnecessary JavaScript and heavy animation libraries.
- Add page titles, meta descriptions and social sharing metadata.
- Add appropriate local SEO information.
- Ensure good loading performance on mobile networks.
- Test on common Android and iPhone screen sizes.
- Keep content such as timings, events, announcements and gallery images easy to update.
- Do not add a backend unless a real requirement needs one.

## 7. Content accuracy and approval

Never fabricate or assume:
- Temple history or founding dates.
- Religious or historical claims.
- Priest names or administration details.
- Darshan and pooja timings.
- Pooja charges or donation amounts.
- Festival dates or event schedules.
- Official phone numbers or social media accounts.
- Bank details, UPI IDs or donation links.

Use clearly marked placeholders until the temple administration confirms the information.

Any donation, online booking, payment or personal-data collection feature must be added only with explicit authorization and verified official details.

## 8. SEO and discoverability

- Use the correct temple name and address consistently.
- Add descriptive page titles and meta descriptions.
- Use meaningful image alt text.
- Add local business/temple structured data only when the details are accurate and the schema is appropriate.
- Make the site shareable through messaging apps and social platforms.
- Ensure the website is indexable and has a sitemap and robots.txt.

## 9. Final quality checklist

Before delivery, verify that:

- The website looks excellent on mobile before desktop.
- All navigation links work.
- Phone and map links open correctly.
- Timings and contact details are verified.
- No placeholder or invented content is presented as fact.
- Images genuinely represent this temple or are clearly labelled as illustrative.
- Text is readable and accessible.
- Pages load quickly on mobile data.
- There is no horizontal scrolling or broken layout.
- The design feels peaceful, devotional, authentic and professionally commissioned.

## Final instruction

Do not deliver a basic template. Build a complete, polished temple website with careful attention to authenticity, devotional atmosphere, mobile usability, typography, spacing, image quality, performance and content accuracy.

The first impression on a visitor’s phone should be peaceful, divine, welcoming and premium. Use actual temple photographs wherever possible, and prioritize temple-approved content over generic imagery.
